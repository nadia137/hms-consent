Eliminate paper forms, clipboards, and lost consent documents with our Digital Patient Consent Management module, designed specifically for Odoo-based Hospital Management Systems (HMS). This powerful tool digitizes the entire patient consent process, from admission and routine procedures to complex surgical approvals. Create standardized consent templates, send them to patients for electronic signature, and automatically link the legally-valid, signed documents directly to the patient's Electronic Health Record (EHR). By providing a secure, auditable, and paperless workflow, you can ensure compliance, reduce administrative workload, and enhance the patient experience from the moment they walk through your door.

Key Features

Seamless EHR Integration

The most critical feature. Every signed consent form is automatically attached directly to the patient's master record in Odoo, creating a single, unified source of truth for all clinical and administrative staff.

Customizable Consent Templates

Build a library of pre-approved templates for every need: Surgical Consent, Admission Forms, HIPAA/Privacy Policy Acknowledgment, Release of Information, Anesthesia Consent, and more.

In-Person & Remote Signing Workflows

Perfect for any scenario. Send a secure link to patients for pre-visit paperwork via email/SMS, or use a tablet at the registration desk or bedside for immediate, in-person signing.

Legally Compliant Electronic Signatures

Capture secure, time-stamped e-signatures that are compliant with major regulations (like HIPAA). The system records a full audit trail, including the signer's IP address and a timestamp, for legal validation.

Centralized Consent Tracking Dashboard

A real-time dashboard provides a clear overview for nurses and administrative staff. Instantly see which consents are pending, signed, or expired for any patient, ensuring no procedure begins without proper authorization.

Automated Request Triggers

Link consent forms to specific procedures or appointment types. When a patient is scheduled for a particular surgery, the system can automatically trigger the request for the corresponding consent form.
